# Getting Started with Zaki Ledger

Welcome 👋 — and thank you for being one of the first to try this.

Zaki Ledger takes the manual typing out of invoice and receipt entry. **You stay in control; it
does the grunt work.**

## What it does (in one line)
Upload an invoice → the AI reads it and drafts the entry with a **confidence score** → you check and
**approve in one click**. Nothing is saved or posted without you.

## Your first invoice — 4 steps
1. **Upload** — click *Upload invoice* and choose a PDF or a photo.
2. **Review** — every field shows a confidence %. Green means the AI is sure; amber/red means
   *please double-check this one*. Edit anything that isn't right.
3. **Approve** — one click. Done.
4. **It learns** — each time you correct something, it remembers for next time (especially per
   supplier). The more you use it, the sharper it gets for *your* clients.

## Good to know
- **Nothing happens without you.** You approve everything — the AI only ever drafts.
- If the maths doesn't add up (subtotal + tax ≠ total), it flags it so a mistake can't slip through.
- Every correction you make is **logged** — that's both your audit trail and what makes it smarter.

## What it is (and isn't)
- ✅ It's a tool to speed up data entry. **You're still the bookkeeper — the judgement is yours.**
- ❌ It's not a replacement for your accounting software. It works *alongside* Xero/QuickBooks
  (posting entries straight into them is coming next).

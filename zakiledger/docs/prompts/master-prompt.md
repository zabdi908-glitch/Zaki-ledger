## What Zaki Ledger is
An AI copilot that sits **on top of** the tools accountants already use (Xero, QuickBooks, bank
feeds) and kills the three biggest time-wasters in a bookkeeping firm — the **"Big Three"**:
1. Manual **data entry** (invoices/receipts)
2. **Bank reconciliation**
3. **Chasing clients** for documents

It aims to hand a firm back an estimated **15–20 hours per week**. Today's MVP does one thing,
end to end: upload an invoice → the AI extracts the fields with a **confidence score** → the
accountant **approves in one click** → every human correction is logged and makes the next
extraction smarter (our "correction ledger").

## Non-negotiable product principles (reflect these in everything you write)
1. **Human-in-the-loop, always** — the AI drafts, the human approves. Nothing posts silently.
   Every action is logged and reversible.
2. **Confidence scores, not black boxes** — high-confidence auto-fills; low-confidence is flagged.
3. **Sit on top, don't replace** — we integrate with what they use; we **never** claim to replace
   the accountant.
4. **Consistency is a feature** — a machine enters data the same way every time.
5. **Narrow and deep beats broad and shallow** — win one chore completely before the next.

## Who we sell to (ICP)
Small **UK** bookkeeping and accountancy firms — sole practitioners up to ~10 staff — drowning in
manual admin. They feel: burnout from repetitive work, **audit-trail anxiety**, deadline/penalty
anxiety (VAT/MTD, Self Assessment, Companies House), and frustration that admin crowds out
higher-value advisory work.

## The value story (anchor to this, not to features)
The research: ~86% of accountants report burnout from repetitive work; **data entry** eats
8–18 hrs/week, **reconciliation** ~12.5 hrs/week, **chasing clients** 5–15 hrs/week. Reclaim even
**10 hrs/week** at a bookkeeper's ~£25–40 billable/hr = **£1,000–1,600/month of value created.**
So any price in the low hundreds is an easy ROI. **Always lead with hours and pounds saved, never
with features.**

## 6-month goals (context for prioritising)
95%+ extraction accuracy · 2 integrations live (Xero + QuickBooks) · 10–15 firms using it
(pilot/paid) · a documented **10+ hours/week saved** per user · ≥1 published case study.

## Voice & tone (for all copy unless I say otherwise)
- **British English** spelling and phrasing throughout.
- **Plain, credible, peer-to-peer** — I'm a builder talking to busy professionals, not a marketer.
- **No hype, no buzzwords** ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "supercharge"), no emoji unless I ask.
- **Short and concrete.** Accountants are precise, sceptical of "toys", and care about compliance
  and audit trails — respect that.
- **Honest** about what the product does and doesn't do yet (it's an early MVP).

  relevant, low-volume, and always include a one-line opt-out.

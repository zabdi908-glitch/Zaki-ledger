# Plain-English Glossary (for discovery calls)

> So no bookkeeping term ever catches you out on a call. You don't need to be an accountant — you
> need to understand their world enough to talk about the pain. Skim this before calls.

## The software they use
- **Xero** — cloud accounting software (NZ-origin, huge in the UK). Where a business's books live.
- **QuickBooks / QBO** — cloud accounting software by Intuit (US giant, big in UK). "QBO" = the
  online version. Xero's main rival.
- **Sage** — older UK accounting software; still common, especially with established firms.
- **FreeAgent** — cloud accounting popular with freelancers/small firms (owned by NatWest).
- **ProAdvisor / certified advisor** — a bookkeeper certified as an expert in a platform (e.g. a
  "QuickBooks ProAdvisor" — that's what Becky is). A badge of credibility.
- **Dext / Apron / Hubdoc** — receipt & invoice *capture* tools (snap a receipt, it pulls the data).
  ⚠️ Note: these overlap with part of what Zaki Ledger does — useful to know as reference points
  and partial competitors. Our edge: confidence scores + the learning loop + one-click approve.

## Core bookkeeping
- **Bookkeeping vs accounting** — bookkeeping = recording the day-to-day transactions; accounting =
  interpreting them (reports, advice, tax). Our users are mostly bookkeepers.
- **Ledger / general ledger** — the master record of all a business's financial transactions.
- **Chart of accounts** — the list of categories transactions get sorted into (e.g. "office
  supplies", "travel"). "Coding" an invoice = choosing its category.
- **Trial balance** — a checkpoint report that totals everything to check it balances.
- **Reconciliation ("bank rec")** — matching the bank statement to the records, line by line, to
  make sure everything agrees. Tedious and time-consuming. **← Big Three #2 (our Month-4 feature).**
- **Invoice** — a bill (money owed, usually B2B). **Receipt** — proof of a payment already made.
- **Data entry** — keying the details off invoices/receipts into the software. **← Big Three #1 —
  this is exactly what Zaki Ledger automates.**
- **Credit control** — chasing customers for unpaid invoices. **Doc chasing** — chasing clients for
  their paperwork. **← Big Three #3.**
- **Accruals / prepayments** — accounting adjustments for timing. (You rarely need the detail — just
  know they're routine adjustments.)

## UK tax & compliance (the anxiety drivers)
- **HMRC** — His Majesty's Revenue & Customs — the UK tax authority.
- **VAT** — Value Added Tax (usually 20%). Most VAT-registered businesses file a **VAT return**
  every quarter.
- **MTD (Making Tax Digital)** — HMRC's rules requiring businesses to keep **digital** records and
  file via software (not paper/spreadsheets). A big driver of cloud-software adoption.
- **Self Assessment** — the annual personal tax return sole traders/self-employed file (deadline
  31 Jan). A yearly scramble — real pain point.
- **Corporation Tax** — tax on a limited company's profits.
- **Companies House** — the UK registry where companies file accounts and info.
- **CIS (Construction Industry Scheme)** — special tax rules for construction subcontractors.
  (Several of your prospects mention CIS.)
- **Payroll / PAYE / RTI** — paying employees, deducting tax (PAYE), and reporting it to HMRC in
  real time (RTI). **Auto-enrolment** = the workplace-pension duty.
